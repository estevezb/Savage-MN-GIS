Section 1: Overview

Originator:Remediation Division, Minnesota Pollution Control Agency

Title: Minnesota Groundwater Contamination Atlas - Sites

Abstract: This is a point layer showing the sites included in the Minnesota Groundwater Contamination Atlas. The Atlas was first published in June 2020. It shows where testing monitoring wells shows groundwater areas of contamination.

Purpose: This is meant to be a high level search layer. The point is scalable at the state level and gives a general indication of where a groundwater contamination area of concern may be mapped.

Time Period of Content Date: 01/12/2022

Currentness Reference: The data receive daily updates to reflect the most current information available. In aggregate, the data were collected throughout the existence of MPCA’s Remediation Program. Individual records are collected throughout the life-cycle of a Remediation Program interest. Several date fields contained in the data describe when an individual record was last collected or the location information was modified.
