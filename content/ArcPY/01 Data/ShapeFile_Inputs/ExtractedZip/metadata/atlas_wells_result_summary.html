Section 1: Overview

Originator:Remediation Division, Minnesota Pollution Control Agency

Title: Minnesota Groundwater Contamination Atlas - Wells Contamination Results Summary

Abstract: Well locations that are included in the Minnesota Groundwater Contamination Atlas. The Atlas was first published in June 2020. It shows where testing monitoring wells shows groundwater areas of contamination.

Purpose: Analytical data included in the table include summary results at the well. Information defines the wells into the following classes: Contamination above health based guidance, Contamination below health based guidance, Contamination was not detected, or Well with no data from the last five years.

Time Period of Content Date: 01/12/2022

Currentness Reference: The data receive daily updates to reflect the most current information available. In aggregate, the data were collected throughout the existence of MPCA’s Remediation Program. Individual records are collected throughout the life-cycle of a Remediation Program interest. Several date fields contained in the data describe when an individual record was last collected or the location information was modified.
