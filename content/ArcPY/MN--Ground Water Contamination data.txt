MN--Ground Water Contamination data mapping and analysis
 
ArcGIS Workflow (ArcGIS v.3.3)
------------------
Datasets
1. Groundwater and PFAS datasets 6 and 2 feature classes, respectively
	- 6 fc MN GroundWater Contamination Atlas (.shp files)
	- 2 fc MN PFAS levels (spreadsheets converted to points)
2. MN City/township layer (https://webgis.dot.state.mn.us/65agsf1/rest/services/sdw_govnt/CITY_TOWNSHIP_UNORG_TERR/FeatureServer/0)


Workflow
1. Spatial join :Add city/township to contamination layer atlas 
contamination summary layer , intersect, one to one --MN_PFAS_SpatialJoin

2. Summary Statistic, Max Date: to get most recent water sampling for each city/township as a standalone table

3. Create Field : Add field called join_key (TEXT) to the Max Date table, !Feature_NAME!+"_"+ str(!MaxDate!)
4. Create Field: Repeat for MN_PFAS_SpatialJoin  !Feature_NAME!+"_"+str(!Latestsam!)

5. Select by Attribute: to get latest PFAS by city/township  input- MN_PFAS_SpatialJoin, SQL expression:join_key IN (SELECT join_key FROM MN_PFAS_Levels_Max_date)

6. Summarize within: to get the highest PFAS exceedance value across multiple samples for the latest sampling date, input_poly-City/township, input_features - MN_PFAS_SpatialJoin, use the selected records, summary field: exceedance max
